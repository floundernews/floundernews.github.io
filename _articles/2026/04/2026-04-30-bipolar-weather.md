Toronto residents are raising concerns as the weather in the city begins behaving in a way that feels strangely familiar, especially to those who know the rhyme. Conditions have been switching from rain to sunshine and even snow within minutes, leaving people confused and completely unprepared for the day.

Many residents have begun to notice a pattern in this unusual behaviour. We spoke to Lindsey Garber, a teacher who grew up in Toronto, who said she has “never seen anything like this in all her years here,” adding that “it feels like we are following a nursery rhyme: It’s raining, it’s pouring, the old man is snoring… It’s sunny, it’s shining, the old man is whining.” Another long-time resident said he no longer checks the forecast and instead “just waits to see which line of the rhyme we’re on.” Children around the city have begun giving warnings on clothing for the weather, as they know the rhyme the best. Even local wildlife is unprepared with birds beginning migration in the morning, and coming back out just 3 hours later. City weather reporters are now warning residents to carry multiple outfits at all times as weather conditions continue to shift rapidly without warning.

As theories about this “Mystery Weather Man” spread online, residents have begun to take matters into their own hands. It is reported that people have been banging pots outside their houses to prevent the old man from snoring, while others have yelled sad stories in hopes of extending periods of rain. Toronto police have asked everyone to “stop interfering with the old man” until more information is available. At the time of reporting it is currently raining, though sunshine is expected within minutes.The status of the old man remains unknown.
