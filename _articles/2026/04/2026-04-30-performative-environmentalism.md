Beloved reader,

We here at the Flounder care deeply for the environment. We find it terribly sad that Mother Nature is feeling down in the dumps– literally, since she’s being buried under mountains of trash way bigger than you may think. For scale, those mountains are somehow larger than the pile of laundry on that one chair in your room (you know the one we’re talking about). They’re larger even than the awkward silence after one of your jokes doesn’t land right.

\*cough\*

Now, that being said, that doesn’t mean that she is without help. This impending crisis has made people all over the world come together with their bold, slightly jumbled, inspiring, and incredibly misguided ideas and do their part in “helping” the Earth. In fact, here are some examples of things people have done, just this week, to put their own conscience at ease:

1. Posted an AI-generated photo of a sad polar bear with the caption “It’s their planet too”. Recent records show an exponential spike in polar bear happiness, and experts say it’s all because of this post. Congratulations, @rsmith91, you’ve changed the world\!
2. Used a sixth straw for one drink at the movies after the first five dissolved, then refrained from complaining about the papery taste of their drink and congratulated themselves on it for the rest of the day. ‘Cause really, what better way to help the environment than to create more “recyclable” waste?
3. Bought their 11th reusable water bottle in six weeks, single-handedly keeping Owala in business and ensuring they never have to reuse the other ten.
4. Recycled their plastic Caesar salad container without washing it out (“The dressing will decompose, right? So it’s basically recyclable”). They may have inadvertently poisoned a stray dog rooting through the trash, but at least they can give themselves a big pat on the back for doing their part.
5. Bought 25 reusable bags at the grocery store to reduce their future waste. Spoiler alert: they’ll forget to bring every single one of them with them next time.
6. Switched to all possible eco-friendly products, throwing out everything they already have. After all, nothing says “eco-friendly” like tossing everything you own in the garbage.
7. Asked ChatGPT: “What are some ways I can help the planet? Also, can you write this essay for me?” After a few seconds of deep thought, they decided thinking is too hard and maybe AI should just do it for them.
8. Drove across the city in rush-hour traffic to attend a Save the Planet rally. They then found that it was too hot outside and chose to drive along the rally route with the air conditioning blasting. Greenhouse gases? Never heard of ‘em.
9. Brought a hose to a river near their house to “fill it back up”. Sure, ‘cause that’s how it works.
10. Left their fan on overnight for the whole week to generate “renewable wind energy”. This one was, to put it frankly, really disappointing. For us, and for them, when they get their electricity bill at the end of the month.
11. Turned off the lights for Earth Day while ordering online groceries, dog food, that one new shirt they’ve had their eye on for two weeks, and \$100 worth of Temu technology. Those “ethically” sourced microchips are definitely environmentally sound… right?
12. Set their belongings on fire to protest consumerism. While they may have gotten arrested for arson, they sent a powerful message: “We the people are perfectly capable of creating air pollution all on our own.”

So you see, dear reader, the world is not entirely hopeless. With this level of dedication from heroes like the ones on this list, the world as we know it won’t end till next Tuesday. Wednesday if we’re lucky.
