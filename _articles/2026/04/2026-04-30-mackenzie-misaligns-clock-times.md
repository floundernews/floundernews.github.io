MACKENZIE \- Many students have been complaining about the incorrect clocks around the school, which don't seem to exactly align with Eastern time. Some classrooms have read 4 minutes earlier, 2 minutes later, or even 40 minutes off in severe cases. While this may seem like simple human error in setting clock times, Flounder reporters have discovered that this is actually an intentional policy designed to create time zones within the school.

Around the world, time zones are set at around 15 degrees of longitude apart, at 1 hour differences between adjacent time zones. Thus, if one were located at the border of a certain time zone, they could be transported 1 hour into the past or future\! This seems quite ridiculous, right?

The school seems to have come up with a solution for this folly. Creating time zones in Mackenzie is designed to create a more proportional and accurate time depending on location within the school.

As you learnt in grade 9 geography class, in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) every degree of longitude (roughly 80 km in Toronto) equates to around 4 minutes of difference. However, given that the school isn’t that large, they may have miscalculated these different times.

After this time zone plan surfaced, many students reached out with complaints. Grade 12 student Layt Foklas stated: “The fact that they didn't share the reason behind this shows that they know it's nonsense too\! Even if I leave 5 minutes early for my first class, I'll be late for my second…”

However we did find one student in support of the school's misaligned clocks. An anonymous student has stated, “My classes are aligned in just a way where I have 10 minute breaks between classes\! I say let the system stay.”

The big question still stands; who was responsible for this? While we don't have solid evidence, many of our Flounder journalists have suspected that the Lyon newspaper may have had a part. There have been several reports of Lyon issues having severely postponed release dates and little action in the school year. Given the carelessness with how the clock changes have impacted students’ punctuality, it would make sense for the perpetrators to not have such a regard for punctuality themselves.

It is currently unclear whether this system will continue. While there was a meeting to discuss future action, the majority of people didn't show up at the scheduled time. Thus, it's safe to say that any decisions regarding the school times will be severely delayed.
